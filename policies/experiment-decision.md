# Experiment decision policy

Choose the next experiment by this order:

1. Preserve the verified champion.
2. Eliminate candidates that violate data, budget, permission, or comparability constraints.
3. Prefer a mechanism-backed, one-variable test from an underexplored angle.
4. Require a prediction and falsifiable gate before compute.
5. After a failure, record symptom → likely mechanism → affected component.
6. Permit at most one targeted repair of the same idea unless the budget is explicitly expanded.
7. Stop on the frozen budget, repeated infrastructure failure, no useful untried lever, or the declared no-improvement limit.

This policy chooses experiments; it does not make broader product or organizational strategy decisions.
